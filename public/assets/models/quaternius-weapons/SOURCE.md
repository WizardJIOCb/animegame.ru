# Quaternius Toon Shooter weapons

The pistol, rifle, rocket launcher and sniper models come from the
[Quaternius Toon Shooter Game Kit](https://quaternius.com/packs/toonshootergamekit.html).
The laser weapon uses the pack's `ShortCannon` model with an emissive material in-game.

The pack is published under CC0 1.0. A copy of the CC0 legal code is included in
`CC0-1.0-Legal-Code.txt`.
